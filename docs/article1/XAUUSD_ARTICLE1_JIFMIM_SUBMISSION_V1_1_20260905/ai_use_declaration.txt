Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process

During the preparation of this work, the author used OpenAI ChatGPT to assist with manuscript organization, language editing, clarity, consistency checking, literature-source verification, and preparation of LaTeX submission files. The author reviewed and edited all assisted content, verified the reported results and cited sources against the research records and original publications, retained control over the scientific arguments and interpretations, and takes full responsibility for the content of the published article. OpenAI ChatGPT was also used as a support tool in parts of the broader program for code drafting, code review, diagnostic reasoning, and preparation of reproducible tables and figures; model identifiers and access dates are retained in the project interaction records. It did not autonomously select scientific claims, alter frozen observations, or approve any result. All code and outputs used for this article were subject to human review, locked-input checks, deterministic seeds where applicable, automated tests, and an independent output-level audit. The article figures were generated directly from frozen CSV tables with the supplied deterministic Python, pandas, and Matplotlib workflow; no generative image model was used.
