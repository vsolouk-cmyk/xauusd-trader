# XAUUSD Article 1 — Manuscript Foundation V1.0

Date: 4 September 2026  
Status: Evidence-audited manuscript foundation  
Study classification: Retrospective systematic audit with a prospectively locked consolidated rerun

## 1. Editorial decision

The paper should not be framed as a search for a winning XAUUSD strategy. Its publishable contribution is the controlled dismantling of apparent backtest profitability across a heterogeneous, economically motivated strategy universe. The central result is not that every gold strategy fails. It is that positive average returns observed during a long research program did not become supportable trading effects when evaluated under a locked combination of realistic costs, familywise multiplicity control, source-native acceptance gates, temporal sensitivity analysis, and cross-feed replication where feasible.

This distinction is decisive. A manuscript that leads with the absence of a winning strategy will read as a project report. A manuscript that studies how candidate profitability decays across increasingly credible evidentiary layers can make a broader contribution to empirical finance, technical-trading research, and reproducible strategy evaluation.

## 2. Locked working title

**From Apparent Edge to No-Go: A Point-in-Time, Cost-Aware Audit of Technical, Event, Cross-Asset, Machine-Learning, and Relative-Value Strategies in XAUUSD**

Shorter submission alternative, if required by the target journal:

The first title is preferable during drafting because it communicates breadth. The shorter alternative may be more effective at submission if the full paper demonstrates the strategy taxonomy in Table 1.

## 3. Article type and intended contribution

The manuscript is an empirical finance paper with a reproducible-computational component. It is not primarily a software paper, a machine-learning paper, or a claim of prospective experimental validation.

Its contribution has four parts:

1. **Breadth under a common evidence standard.** The study consolidates 39 primary specifications across 20 economic families rather than reporting a single selected strategy.
2. **An explicit profitability-attrition analysis.** It measures how the set of apparently positive strategies contracts as gross returns are replaced by normal, severe, and fixed-stress cost assumptions.
3. **Joint statistical and operational credibility checks.** It combines source-native performance gates with familywise max-t inference, deflated Sharpe diagnostics, alignment-window sensitivity, and cross-feed replication where the data contract permits it.
4. **Complete retention of negative evidence.** Failed specifications, unsupported families, non-applicable replication tests, and the principled non-estimability of probability of backtest overfitting are preserved rather than converted into selective positive narratives.

Novelty should be claimed at the level of this integrated design and its application to a broad XAUUSD research program. The manuscript should not claim that individual statistical procedures are new.

## 4. Research questions

Because historical outcomes had been examined before the final registry was frozen, the paper should use research questions rather than post hoc confirmatory hypotheses.

**RQ1.** How rapidly does apparent profitability among heterogeneous XAUUSD strategy specifications decline as increasingly conservative transaction-cost assumptions are applied?

**RQ2.** Do any pre-specified economic families retain statistical support after source-native acceptance gates and familywise multiple-testing correction are imposed?

**RQ3.** Do the strongest remaining specifications exhibit temporal stability and cross-feed reproducibility where those tests are contractually applicable?

**RQ4.** Which failure mechanisms—cost sensitivity, weak familywise evidence, insufficient sample size, temporal concentration, benchmark underperformance, or feed dependence—account for the rejection of apparently promising specifications?

RQ4 is the manuscript's strongest analytical opportunity. It converts a collection of null results into a structured account of why apparent edges fail.

## 5. Main scientific statement

The principal claim is:

> No robust edge was detected among the 39 locked primary specifications, representing 20 economic families, under the pre-specified XAUUSD data, reference periods, transaction-cost assumptions, multiplicity correction, stability requirements, and cross-feed evidence standard.

The following stronger statements are not supported:

- all XAUUSD strategies are unprofitable;
- the gold market is universally efficient;
- the entire research program was outcome-blind or fully prospective;
- 28,685 normalized trade records constitute 28,685 independent experiments;
- every specification received an independent cross-feed test; or
- the audit independently reconstructed all signals from the original vendor databases.

## 6. Draft abstract

Backtested trading strategies are commonly evaluated after extensive specification search, creating a material risk that apparently attractive returns reflect transaction-cost omissions, selection effects, unstable samples, or feed-specific signals. We conduct a point-in-time, cost-aware audit of a heterogeneous XAUUSD research program using a prospectively locked consolidated rerun. The registry contains 47 specifications, of which 39 primary specifications spanning 20 economic families were recomputed; eight machine-learning combinations were retained for forensic documentation only. Primary inference is confined to the pre-2025 reference period, prohibits historical strategy-result files as computational inputs, and evaluates source-native severe-cost outcomes. Familywise evidence is assessed by a centered circular moving-block max-t bootstrap, supplemented by source-native acceptance gates, deflated Sharpe ratios, a pre-declared alignment-warning sensitivity test, and cross-feed replication where applicable. The primary specifications generated 28,685 normalized trade or rebalance records. Twenty-four specifications had positive mean gross returns, compared with nine under normal costs, five under severe costs, and one under a common fixed-stress schedule. No specification passed its complete source-native gate set, and none of the 20 families was supported. The smallest familywise adjusted p-value was 0.446, while the highest deflated-Sharpe probability was 0.011. Of 20 cross-feed-eligible specifications, seven failed, including two whose severe-cost effect changed sign. These findings do not establish the absence of all possible gold-market predictability. They show that, within the locked strategy universe, apparent profitability did not survive the joint economic, statistical, temporal, and data-source requirements necessary for a defensible trading-edge claim.

Word count: approximately 225.

## 8. Manuscript architecture

Target main-text length before journal formatting: 7,500–8,500 words, excluding references and online supplement.

### 1. Introduction — 900–1,100 words

The introduction should proceed through five moves:

1. Explain why strategy discovery creates a credibility problem when many economically related rules are evaluated on overlapping data.
2. Show that transaction costs and multiple testing are not independent afterthoughts; they alter which strategies appear attractive.
3. Identify the XAUUSD-specific gap: existing work tends to study a narrower rule universe, a particular market venue, or a single methodological layer.
4. State the study design honestly: a retrospective program followed by a prospectively locked consolidated rerun.
5. State the result and contribution without withholding the null conclusion.

The introduction should end with a compact contribution paragraph and a roadmap. It should not begin with a generic description of gold's economic importance.

### 2. Related literature and research gap — 1,100–1,300 words

Organize the literature into four conversations:

- data snooping, false discoveries, and multiple testing in trading-rule research;
- transaction costs, persistence, and out-of-sample degradation;
- technical and timing evidence in foreign exchange, commodities, and gold;
- reproducibility diagnostics, including deflated Sharpe ratios and backtest-overfitting concerns.

The gap is not merely that few papers study XAUUSD. The defensible gap is that prior strands are rarely integrated into a single locked audit spanning technical, event, cross-asset, machine-learning, time-series-momentum, and relative-value families while retaining the entire negative result set.

### 3. Data, chronology, and provenance — 900–1,100 words

Required subsections:

3.1 Market-data sources and economic role of each feed  
3.2 Point-in-time reference interval and exclusion of post-2024 outcomes  
3.3 Timestamp normalization, DST contract, and the October 2019 alignment warning  
3.4 Cross-feed routing and replication eligibility  
3.5 Data limitations and non-independence of overlapping specification-level trades

The primary data-contract interval is 2 January 2015 at 07:00 UTC through 1 January 2025 at 00:00 UTC, with the endpoint excluded. Source-block-specific availability and inclusion rules must be reported rather than implying that every specification covers the entire interval.

### 4. Locked strategy universe and evaluation protocol — 1,500–1,800 words

Required subsections:

4.1 Registry construction and separation of 39 primary from 8 forensic-only specifications  
4.2 Economic-family taxonomy  
4.3 Trade inclusion and return normalization  
4.4 Gross, normal, severe, and fixed-stress transaction-cost definitions  
4.5 Source-native acceptance gates  
4.6 Familywise max-t bootstrap  
4.7 Cross-feed replication criteria  
4.8 Alignment-warning sensitivity  
4.9 Deflated Sharpe diagnostic  
4.10 Why probability of backtest overfitting was not estimated

The family support rule must be expressed as a conjunction, not as a menu of optional tests. A family is supported only when at least one specification passes its source-native gates, the familywise adjusted p-value is no greater than 0.05, no applicable alignment fragility is observed, the applicable cross-feed gate passes, and no provenance or point-in-time violation exists.

### 5. Results — 1,500–1,800 words

Recommended order:

5.1 Attrition of positive mean returns across cost layers  
5.2 Source-native gate outcomes  
5.3 Familywise multiplicity results  
5.4 Forensic analysis of the strongest positive severe-cost specifications  
5.5 Cross-feed replication and feed dependence  
5.6 Alignment sensitivity  
5.7 Deflated Sharpe results and PBO non-estimability

The Results section should lead with the 24 → 9 → 5 → 1 attrition pattern, followed immediately by the finding that zero specifications passed the complete source-native gates and zero families received support. The discussion of the best-performing rules must explain failure causes rather than celebrate rank order.

### 6. Discussion — 800–1,000 words

The discussion should distinguish three mechanisms:

1. **Economic attrition:** costs eliminate most positive averages.
2. **Statistical attrition:** the remaining positive effects are weak relative to familywise search.
3. **Reproducibility attrition:** selected results may depend on small samples or the primary feed.

The broader interpretation is that strategy evaluation should be treated as an evidence-convergence problem. A positive mean is only one layer and is insufficient by itself.

### 7. Limitations and scope of inference — 450–600 words

This section must state:

- retrospective exposure to historical outcomes before the final lock;
- output-level rather than raw-data-level independent audit;
- heterogeneous calendars, horizons, and objectives;
- overlapping, non-independent specification-level records;
- incomplete cross-feed applicability;
- broker-proxy and venue-specific limitations;
- the bounded nature of all no-edge conclusions; and
- the inability to estimate PBO without violating its design assumptions.

### 8. Conclusion — 300–450 words

The conclusion should return to evidence attrition and research governance. It should not suggest that a profitable strategy is likely to emerge from minor parameter changes. Any future work must use new data, a genuinely new hypothesis, or a different asset universe rather than reopen the locked universe after observing its outcome.

## 12. Journal strategy as of 4 September 2026

All choices remain provisional until the current JCR category quartile and every author charge are verified through the institutional JCR portal and the journal's submission system immediately before submission.

### First target: Journal of International Financial Markets, Institutions and Money

This is the strongest subject-matter fit. It has published directly relevant work on intraday technical trading in China's gold market and on false-discovery-controlled technical analysis. The publisher currently describes the journal as supporting open access and reports a 6.0 Impact Factor and 11.0 CiteScore. The paper must be framed as international financial-market evidence and research governance, not as retail trading-system documentation.

### Second target: International Review of Financial Analysis

This is the more ambitious target. It has published a directly relevant gold market-timing study using multiple-testing-aware analysis. The publisher currently reports a 10.2 Impact Factor and 13.8 CiteScore and describes the journal as supporting open access. The editorial risk is that a single-instrument null result may be judged too narrow unless the manuscript foregrounds the general evidence-convergence framework and the unusually broad strategy taxonomy.

### Third target: Journal of Empirical Finance

This is a methodologically credible fallback, particularly if the paper emphasizes formal empirical discipline and complete negative evidence. The publisher currently reports a 2.5 Impact Factor and 3.8 CiteScore and describes the journal as supporting open access. The current JCR quartile and the absence of all mandatory author charges require verification before submission.

### Reserve target: Quantitative Finance

The journal publishes empirical quantitative-finance research and operates as a hybrid Open Select journal. It is a plausible destination if the manuscript gives greater prominence to the locked computational protocol and statistical design. It is less attractive than the first two targets if the empirical gold-market contribution remains the main narrative.

### Excluded target: Research in International Business and Finance

The journal announced in July 2026 that it is moving to a fully open-access model. It should therefore be removed from the target list unless the transition terms explicitly preserve a no-fee publication route or a full waiver is confirmed in writing. A possible waiver is not an acceptable planning assumption.
