# Recent-literature update and integration record

Date of audit: 5 September 2026

## Scope and decision rule

The revision did not treat recency as a numerical target in isolation. A new source was retained only when it sharpened one of four arguments used by the manuscript: large-universe technical-rule evidence; the distinction between forecast accuracy and executable trading performance; the state-dependent role of gold; or the continuing relevance of costs, temporal instability, and data-snooping control. Every added source is cited in the manuscript body.

## Sections rewritten

- Introduction: current technical-trading evidence, current gold-state dependence, and the forecast-to-trading distinction.
- Related literature: a new subsection on gold forecasting and the prediction-to-trading gap; updated technical-rule and gold-investment synthesis.
- Discussion: explicit comparison with recent positive and negative findings, with market- and estimand-specific limits.

The foundational sources were retained because the statistical procedures used in the paper originate in that literature. Recency was added around them, not used as a reason to replace the primary methodological citations with secondary summaries.
