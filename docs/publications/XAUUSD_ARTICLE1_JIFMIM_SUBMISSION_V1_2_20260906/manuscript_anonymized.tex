\begin{abstract}
Trading strategies are often reported after many specifications have been explored, leaving costs, selection, sample instability, and feed dependence only partly visible. We examine these issues in a retrospective audit of a heterogeneous XAUUSD research program, followed by a consolidated rerun specified before execution. The registry contains 47 strategies. Thirty-nine primary specifications from 20 economic families were recomputed, while eight historically selected machine-learning combinations were retained as forensic records and excluded from primary inference. The runner could not read earlier strategy results, used source-native cost schedules, and ended every reference sample in 2024. Inference combines a centered circular moving-block max-$t$ bootstrap with source-native acceptance criteria, deflated Sharpe ratios, a pre-declared timestamp sensitivity, and cross-feed replication where applicable. The primary specifications produced 28,685 normalized trade or rebalance records. Positive means fell from 24 before costs to 9 under normal costs, 5 under severe costs, and 1 under a common fixed-stress schedule. Every specification failed at least one native criterion, and adjusted evidence remained weak across all 20 families: the minimum familywise $p$-value was 0.446 and the largest deflated-Sharpe probability was 0.011. Seven of the 20 specifications eligible for cross-feed evaluation failed, including two sign reversals. These findings are confined to the registered universe. Within that boundary, apparent profitability did not survive the combined economic, statistical, temporal, and data-source requirements of a defensible trading edge.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Severe costs reduced positive strategy means from 24 to 5.
\item All 39 strategies failed at least one source-native acceptance test.
\item The minimum familywise adjusted $p$-value was 0.446.
\item Seven of 20 eligible strategies failed cross-feed replication.
\item Complete failure records show where apparent profitability was lost.
\end{highlights}
