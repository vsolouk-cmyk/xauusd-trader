# Stage55 Forward Frequency and Alternative Path Readiness

Stage55 is a non-trading planning/reporting stage. It answers one operational question: if Stage51/52 is valid but low-frequency, how long might it take to collect enough true-forward evidence, and should alternative thesis designs be prepared in parallel?

It does not promote any strategy and it does not authorize EA, paper-live, live trading, or order submission.

Historical Stage51 trade counts are treated only as a cadence prior. They are not forward evidence and must not be used to pass Stage53 gates.
