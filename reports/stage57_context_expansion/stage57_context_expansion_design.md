# Stage57 Context Expansion Design

Stage57 is a no-promotion preparation stage. It is used after Stage56 produced no hard-audit survivors across the three price-action-only alternative families.

The strategic change is not to rescue Stage56. Stage56 alternatives should be archived if they have no hard-audit pass. Stage51/52 remains active as a passive true-forward evidence collector, while the next active research branch expands context beyond pure price action.

Candidate context layers:

1. Session and spread regime derived from AMarkets broker data.
2. News blackout / economic calendar windows.
3. USD and US-rate proxy context if CSVs are available.
4. Weekly COT positioning as slow regime context.
5. Broker-reference basis/data-quality regime labels.

No EA, paper-live, live trading, promotion, or order submission is authorized.
